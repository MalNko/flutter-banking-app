# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment for all contributors, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and inclusive
- Welcome diverse perspectives
- Focus on constructive criticism
- Respect confidentiality
- Report inappropriate behavior
- Follow all security guidelines

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language
- Publishing private information without consent
- Intentional disruption of discussions
- Disclosing security vulnerabilities publicly
- Conduct that violates professional standards
- Any behavior that makes others feel unsafe

## Reporting

Report violations to: conduct@banking-app.com

All reports will be reviewed confidentially and investigated.
